**OverRec v1.1.1** is out. Besides OBS, you can now choose **NVIDIA App** as the recorder.

<!-- truncate -->

## What is new

- **Recording with NVIDIA App**: start and stop NVIDIA App's recording, save a replay and take screenshots from the wrist panel and the controller buttons. OverRec sends the hotkeys set in NVIDIA App for you
- **Switching the recorder**: switch between OBS and NVIDIA App in "Connection" in the PC settings, or under "Recorder" in the panel's gear
- **A panel for NVIDIA App**: with NVIDIA App, the panel has only the three buttons that work
- **First-run guide**: choose the recorder in the first step. With NVIDIA App, the OBS steps are skipped
- **Pointer**: the dot pointing at the panel shakes less

NVIDIA App does not tell other apps whether it is recording, so no recording sign is shown. See [Recording with NVIDIA App](/nvidia) for how to use it and get it ready.

## Updating

You can check for a new version under "Update" in the settings. Installing over the old one keeps your settings.
