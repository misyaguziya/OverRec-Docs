**OverRec v1.1.0** is out. From the drawer under the panel, you can switch sources and filters from inside VR.

<!-- truncate -->

## What is new

- **Show or hide sources**: press a row to switch a source in the current scene
- **Filters on and off**: switch the filters on a source from a list
- **Inside groups**: work with the sources inside a group too. The heading shows where you are, as "Scene › Group"
- **Studio Mode**: work with the preview scene's sources. What goes out does not change until the transition
- **Position bar**: with many scenes or sources, you can see where you are in the whole list
- **A dot on the preview**: a dot shows where you point, on OBS's picture too

See [Turning sources and filters on and off](/sources) for how to use it.

## Updating

You can check for a new version under "Update" in the settings. Installing over the old one keeps your settings.
