# Customizing

## Position and size {#place}

Grab the panel to put it where you like, at the size you like ([Grabbing and moving the panel](/start/basics#grab)). It stays there the next time you start OverRec.

### Back to wrist {#reset}

For when you lose it, or want its first place back. Either works.

- **"Back to wrist"** in "VR controls" → "Cannot find the wrist panel" in the PC settings window
- **"Back to wrist"** in ⚙ → "Position and size" on the panel

## The small sign {#mini}

When the panel is too big and in the way, make it small. The wrist then shows only what is easy to forget: recording, streaming, muted.

![The small sign, recording, with the mic muted](./img/panel-indicator.png)

- **Make it small**: press the inward diagonal-arrow button at the top right of the panel (pointed at, it says "Make small"). "VR controls" → "Make the wrist panel small" in the PC settings window does the same.
- **Bring the panel back**: point at the small sign, pull the trigger and let go.
- **Position and size**: the same place as the panel. It sits at the panel's bottom edge, centered: the panel rises out of it when it opens. Move or resize either one and the other follows.
- **Preview**: the camera button at its right end shows and hides what OBS shows ([The OBS preview](/preview#small)).

The small sign, too, is shown only while your wrist is turned toward your face.

## Which hand {#hand}

Choose **Left** or **Right** in ⚙ → "Worn on" on the panel. You point and press with the hand opposite the one the panel is on.

When you change hands, the panel appears at its first place on the new wrist.

## Haptic feedback {#haptics}

The controller vibrates when you point at a button, press it, or something is saved. When something goes wrong, it vibrates longer.

Turn it off from ⚙ on the panel or "VR controls" in the PC settings window.

## Controller bindings {#bindings}

You can work OBS straight from controller buttons, without opening the panel. These six can be bound:

- Save replay
- Start / stop recording
- Mute the mic
- Screenshot
- Chapter
- Next scene

Games use the buttons, so **nothing is bound at first**. Bind only what you want to buttons that are free.

1. Start SteamVR.
2. Press **"Open bindings"** in "VR controls" → "Controller bindings" in the PC settings window.
3. In SteamVR's window, choose a button and bind an action to it.

The buttons that press and grab the panel can be changed in the same window.

:::tip
Stopping a recording from a bound button stops it at once, without asking. Bind it to a button you won't press by accident.
:::

## Display language {#language}

Choose it in "General" → "Display language" in the PC settings window. The settings window, the wrist panel and the tray menu use that language.
